Referee Call	Romaji	Meaning
はっけよい / 発気揚々	Hakkeyoi	"Put some spirit into it!" (Shouted at the start or when the action stalls)
残った、残った！	Nokotta, nokotta!	"You're still in it!" / "Still remaining!" (Shouted rapidly while the wrestlers are actively fighting to show the match is ongoing)
勝負あり！	Shōbu ari!	"The match is decided!" (Shouted to signal the end of the bout)
